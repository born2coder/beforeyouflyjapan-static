YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Choose either the Tokyo Tower exterior with Zojoji or a reserved observation-deck visit. If ticket queues are uncertain, enjoy the exterior view and return on schedule.

What to budget

Exterior viewing is free; observation decks require tickets and have different products and prices.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Onarimon, Akabanebashi or Hamamatsucho.

Choose the station used by your return airport route. The tower is not directly on Hamamatsucho's monorail platform; retain the walking allowance.

Open Onarimon, Akabanebashi or Hamamatsucho on the map ↗

Keep collection on the Hamamatsucho return route. Before walking to Onarimon, Akabanebashi or Hamamatsucho, confirm a locker or staffed counter, its closing time and the exact gate side. Local storage prices have not been verified for this stop; use the posted rate rather than assuming the same price as another station. If no suitable space is available, keep the bag and choose a station meal or the airport instead.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A 333-meter broadcasting tower and observation landmark near Zojoji.

What to notice: Before going up, step back and see the full structure from ground level; the tower’s relationship with the surrounding city is part of its appeal.

Highlights
