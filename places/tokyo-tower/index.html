WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A 333-meter broadcasting tower and observation landmark near Zojoji.

What to notice: Before going up, step back and see the full structure from ground level; the tower’s relationship with the surrounding city is part of its appeal.

DEPARTURE-DAY FIT

How to use Tokyo Tower before your flight

Tokyo Tower can be a memorable final landmark before Haneda when visibility is good and your ticket timing is predictable. The tower is visually close to several stations, but approaches include hills and city blocks that make the walk longer than a glance at the skyline suggests.

Choose either an exterior-and-Zojoji view or a paid observation visit as the core. Admission, lifts and crowded decks add variable time. Combining both can work only when the planner margin remains generous after accounting for the walk back to transport.

A practical one-way visit

  1. Approach from the station that best continues toward Hamamatsucho or your Haneda route.
  2. Complete the exterior view or the selected observation experience without adding extra floors or nearby sites.
  3. Descend and leave at the fixed turnaround time, allowing for the walk back downhill or through intersections.

When to skip it

Skip a deck visit when visibility is poor, ticket queues are long, or a tight Narita departure would require crossing the city afterward.

Before you commit

  • Confirm current ticket, last-entry and operating information.
  • Allow for the uphill approach, lifts and security procedures.
  • Treat Zojoji as a separate addition unless there is ample time.
