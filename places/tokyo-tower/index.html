Tokyo Tower: Location, Tickets & Time for a Short Visit

Tokyo Tower is in Shibakoen, not Ginza. Decide whether you want the tower in your photograph or the view from its observation deck: the first can be a short outdoor stop; the second involves a ticket, an ascent and possible queues.

Is this the stop for you?

Suggested time at the place
20–30 minutes outside; 60–90 minutes on the Main Deck — local visit only
Choose it for
An exterior landmark photo or a deliberate observation-deck visit with enough room for the queue.

Choose another stop if: Poor visibility or ticket waiting would use the time intended for the visit and return.

A view of the tower, or a view from it?

For the exterior, choose a public pavement viewpoint near the tower and, if it fits, a short look toward nearby Zojoji. Allow 20–30 minutes locally for photographs rather than joining an admission queue. Keep roads and entrances clear, and do not look for the often-photographed underground parking stairs: the operator asks visitors not to take pictures there.

For the Main Deck, set aside roughly 60–90 minutes locally and add any unusually long queue. The 150m deck costs ¥1,500 for adults on the reviewed tariff. The 250m Top Deck Tour includes the Main Deck and lists adult prices ¥3,300 online/¥3,500 at the counter; its booked slot makes it a separate commitment.

Use an actual exit, then include the walk back

The tower’s official access table lists Akabanebashi Gate at Akabanebashi Station about five minutes away, Kamiyacho exit 1 about seven, Onarimon A 1 about six, Daimon A 6 about ten, and JR Hamamatsucho North Exit about fifteen. These are operator walking estimates, not the total airport journey.

The nearest arrival station may not be your best airport-return station. Hamamatsucho is useful for the monorail corridor, but the tower is not beside the monorail platform. Keep the street walk and station navigation in the route instead of counting only the train ride.

Hours, visibility and the easy exit from the plan

The Main Deck is listed 09:00–23:00, last admission 22:30; crowding can bring entry forward. The Top Deck is listed 09:00–22:45, last tour 22:15, and can close for weather or operational reasons. The exterior visit needs no observation ticket.

If visibility is poor or the ascent queue is long, keep the exterior stop and choose a meal along your return route. Do not buy another attraction ticket merely to justify having travelled here. A comfortable departure after seeing the tower is a better outcome than watching the clock from the lift queue.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Onarimon, Akabanebashi or Hamamatsucho on the map ↗

Tokyo Tower has lockers in FootTown 1F; the dedicated locker page lists ¥100/¥200/¥300 sizes and access from 09:00 to 22:30. The largest listed compartment is 84.3 cm high × 35.5 cm wide × 64.5 cm deep, so a broad suitcase may still not fit. Take your bag when you leave the tower, rather than adding a return trip after visiting another neighbourhood, and follow the hours posted at the bank.
