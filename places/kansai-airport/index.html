YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

For a low-transfer final stop, stay in your departure terminal or Aeroplaza for one seated meal. Sky View is a separate outing with a bus journey, not a walk from the check-in counter.

What to budget

Meals and shopping are extra; check the correct terminal's public and restricted areas.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Kansai-Airport Station and your departure terminal.

Railway passengers arrive by Terminal 1 and Aeroplaza. Terminal 2 needs the official onward access route; include it before choosing a meal.

Open Kansai-Airport Station and your departure terminal on the map ↗

Keep collection on the Kansai Airport return route. Before walking to Kansai-Airport Station and your departure terminal, confirm a locker or staffed counter, its closing time and the exact gate side. Local storage prices have not been verified for this stop; use the posted rate rather than assuming the same price as another station. If no suitable space is available, keep the bag and choose a station meal or the airport instead.
