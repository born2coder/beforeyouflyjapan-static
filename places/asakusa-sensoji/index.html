YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Walk Kaminarimon → Nakamise → Sensoji, then retrace the approach. Buy one ningyo-yaki snack only if the queue fits; skip the river cruise on this compact route.

What to budget

The temple approach is free; food and purchases are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Asakusa Station and Kaminarimon.

Identify your actual Asakusa operator before leaving: the Tobu, Tokyo Metro and Toei stations are separate from the Tsukuba Express station.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A temple district centered on Kaminarimon, Nakamise and Sensoji’s main hall.

What to notice: Move beyond the first photo at Kaminarimon: watch the route change from shopping street to sacred space as the main hall comes into view.

Highlights

  • Kaminarimon and its giant lantern
  • Nakamise approach
  • Incense, main hall and temple grounds
