Hanazono Shrine: a short Shinjuku stop before Haneda

A compact shrine pause amid Shinjuku.

Does it fit?One compact visitAccess & bagsUse my flight time

Quick answer: Allow 190 minutes for HND from Shinjuku, without luggage tasks. Same-area storage adds 35 minutes; collecting bags elsewhere can add more. Stroller or short-walking options, where supported, add 20 minutes. Keep another three hours before your flight for airport procedures.

YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

At Hanazono Shrine, choose the main hall and a short look at the torii-lined side shrine. Festival stalls can transform the space; keep the visit short when an event fills the grounds.

What to budget

The outdoor shrine visit has no general admission ticket; optional offerings and event purchases are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Shinjuku-sanchome Station.

Use the shrine's access map and return to the station used by your airport route. Do not treat a nearby subway entrance as the JR airport connection.

Open Shinjuku-sanchome Station on the map ↗

If your route uses Tokyo Metro Shinjuku-sanchome, the operator's October 2026 listing has lockers priced ¥500 / ¥600 depending on the compartment. These are Metro lockers, not a price promise for JR or another operator. Keep collection on your chosen route and check the posted charging period and gate side.

Three things to notice

  • A compact shrine pause amid Shinjuku.
  • Compare the red sanctuary and city buildings behind it.
  • Notice the torii and quiet approach, without joining a counter queue.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

The planner uses a conservative 9:00–16:00 daylight visiting window, an editorial limit rather than verified counter hours.

Festivals can make this normally compact stop crowded. Skip it if access is restricted.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use the live route via Shinagawa and Keikyu, or Hamamatsucho and the Tokyo Monorail. This 90-minute allowance includes waiting, station access and changes.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Store bags at Shinjuku on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Walk to Hanazono Shrine: 20 minutes.
  2. Visit Hanazono Shrine grounds: 30 minutes.
  3. Return to Shinjuku station area: 20 minutes.
  4. Head to Haneda Airport: 90 minutes.
  5. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 30 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
