ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Akihabara: anime shopping before a flight

For a first Akihabara stop, make Radio Kaikan the destination rather than trying to cover Electric Town. It puts figures, character goods and hobby shops in one building beside the JR station, with an easy decision about when to leave.

A focused figure or character-goods purchase near JR Akihabara.

Choose another stop if: You need to compare rare stock across several buildings, or would arrive before the shops open.

Radio Kaikan: one category, two floors

Start with the building directory, then pick the floor that matches your interest. The reviewed directory lists AmiAmi on 4F, K-BOOKS on 3F and Kaiyodo Hobby Lobby Tokyo on 5F. A figure fan could begin at AmiAmi and add Kaiyodo; someone looking for character goods can keep the visit to K-BOOKS instead.

Use roughly 30–40 minutes for browsing and the rest for payment and getting back outside. That is our suggested local allowance, not a shop service guarantee. Keep packaging size in mind: an attractive figure box may be much harder to carry than a postcard or key ring. Ask before photographing displays.

Arrive on the Electric Town side

Use JR Akihabara’s Electric Town exit and the operator’s map for Radio Kaikan at 1-15-16 Sotokanda. The building is on the station’s Electric Town side; you do not need a long walk along Chuo-dori for this stop. The Hibiya Line and Tsukuba Express approaches are different, so include their walk to the JR side when using those services.

Before going upstairs, note the entrance you will use on the way out. Returning to the station should be the next move after paying, not the beginning of another hunt for a particular shop.

Good in rain; less useful before opening

The main shopping hours are listed as 10:00–20:00; the basement restaurant has a separate schedule. Browsing the shops has no building admission charge, while merchandise prices depend on the item and condition. Compare the actual label rather than assuming every secondhand item is cheaper.

In rain, stay within this building rather than crossing the district with a suitcase. If the desired stock is unavailable, finish with what you found or leave without buying. A last-day visit is a poor moment to promise yourself an exhaustive search.
