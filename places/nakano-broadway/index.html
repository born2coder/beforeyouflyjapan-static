Nakano Broadway collector shopping before Haneda

Two specialist shops for a focused collector visit.

Does it fit?One compact visitAccess & bagsUse my flight time

Quick answer: Allow 275 minutes for HND from Shinjuku, without luggage tasks. Same-area storage adds 35 minutes; collecting bags elsewhere can add more. Stroller or short-walking options, where supported, add 20 minutes. Keep another three hours before your flight for airport procedures.

YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Pick two specialist Mandarake shops or categories before entering Nakano Broadway. Start after your chosen shops open and avoid waiting for a rare item to be located.

What to budget

Building browsing is free; shop opening times differ and purchases are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Nakano Station, north side.

Go through Nakano Sunmall toward Nakano Broadway and return to Nakano Station. The linked departure plan starts in Shinjuku and includes the outward journey to Nakano; change the starting area if you are already elsewhere.

Open Nakano Station, north side on the map ↗

Keep collection on the Shinjuku return route. Before walking to Nakano Station, north side, confirm a locker or staffed counter, its closing time and the exact gate side. Local storage prices have not been verified for this stop; use the posted rate rather than assuming the same price as another station. If no suitable space is available, keep the bag and choose a station meal or the airport instead.

Three things to notice

  • Two specialist shops for a focused collector visit.
  • Compare vintage manga or character goods with modern releases.
  • Notice how each Mandarake section specializes in a different collection.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

Mandarake Nakano shops generally 12:00–20:00; individual specialist sections may differ. Nakano Broadway is about 5 minutes north of JR Nakano.

The route includes a return to Shinjuku before Haneda. Stairs, shop layouts and crowds make it unsuitable for the short-walking filter.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use the live route via Shinagawa and Keikyu, or Hamamatsucho and the Tokyo Monorail. This 90-minute allowance includes waiting, station access and changes.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Store bags at Shinjuku on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Travel from Shinjuku to Nakano Broadway: 45 minutes.
  2. Browse two Mandarake specialist shops: 65 minutes.
  3. Return to Shinjuku station area: 45 minutes.
  4. Head to Haneda Airport: 90 minutes.
  5. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 30 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
