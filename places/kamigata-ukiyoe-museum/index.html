Kamigata Ukiyoe Museum: before your flight

Osaka actor prints and a nearby temple in one compact route.

Quick answer: Allow 250 minutes for KIX from Namba, without luggage tasks. Same-area storage adds 35 minutes; collecting bags elsewhere can add more. Stroller or short-walking options, where supported, add 20 minutes. Keep another three hours before your flight for airport procedures.

Three things to notice

  • Osaka actor prints and a nearby temple in one compact route.
  • Compare dramatic poses and costume details in actor prints.
  • Look for the regional character of Kamigata prints.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

11:00–18:00; last admission 17:30. Mondays closed; a Monday national holiday opens and the following day closes. Holiday exceptions verified through 2027. Adult admission currently ¥700.

The museum occupies multiple floors; a step-free interior route has not been verified. Hozenji is optional and should be skipped if delayed.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use Nankai Namba to Kansai-Airport Station; this 90-minute allowance includes access, waiting and rail travel, rather than the fastest train alone.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Allow 30 minutes for airport station access / Terminal 2 shuttle navigation, plus 20 minutes of spare time. Terminal 1 passengers retain the spare time.

Store bags at Namba on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Walk to Kamigata Ukiyoe Museum: 15 minutes.
  2. Visit Kamigata Ukiyoe Museum: 55 minutes.
  3. Optional short Hozenji grounds visit: 20 minutes.
  4. Return to Namba station area: 20 minutes.
  5. Head to Kansai Airport: 90 minutes.
  6. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 50 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
