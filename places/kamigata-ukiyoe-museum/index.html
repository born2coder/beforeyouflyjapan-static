Kamigata Ukiyoe Museum before a KIX flight

Choose Kamigata Ukiyoe Museum if you would enjoy looking closely at Osaka’s kabuki actor prints. The collection gives the surrounding theatre district a story; it is not a large survey of every kind of Japanese art. Visit the museum first and make nearby Hozenji optional.

Look at a few prints rather than racing every label

Choose one actor portrait and notice the expression, hairstyle and costume. Then compare it with a second print: the appeal is in how an individual performer becomes recognizable through line and pose. A concentrated 45–60 minute visit can feel more satisfying than a fast circuit followed by a rushed extra attraction.

The museum is opposite Hozenji in the narrow Namba streets. On leaving, a brief look at the temple is easy to understand as an optional change of pace, but a full meal or printmaking workshop is a different commitment. Workshops have their own reservation and price conditions.

Find Hozenji from Namba Walk

Osaka City’s facility listing places the entrance about one minute north of Namba Walk’s north-side exit 16; Hozenji’s own directions identify the nearby exit as B16. Use the museum address, 1-6-4 Namba, and the temple frontage as your final landmarks rather than a general Namba station pin.

Keep your return anchored to Nankai Namba if taking its airport railway. The gallery occupies multiple floors, and the city facility record does not document a special barrier-free arrangement. This guide does not treat its interior as a confirmed step-free route.

Admission, last entry and a no-ticket alternative

The reviewed municipal listing gives 11:00–18:00, with last admission at 17:30. Mondays are normally closed; when the Monday is a holiday, the closure moves to the following day. Listed admission is ¥700 for adults, ¥500 for high-school students and ¥300 for elementary/junior-high pupils; qualifying preschool children are free.

If closed or if you would only have a few rushed minutes inside, use the outdoor Hozenji-and-alley visit instead. Do not buy a ticket on the assumption that you can compensate by shortening the return to the airport train.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Namba Station, Hozenji area on the map ↗

For a Nankai airport return, n・e・s・t at Nankai Namba is one staffed option. Osaka's tourism-board map places it on 2F and lists ¥800 per item per day, 09:00–21:00. Its published limits are a three-side total up to 220 cm and weight up to 30 kg; perishables are excluded. Return for your bag before the counter closes, then continue to the airport train. This listing is dated 1 October 2025, so the counter's current terms take priority.
