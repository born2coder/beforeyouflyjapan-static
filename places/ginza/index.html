ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Ginza shopping before a Haneda flight

For useful last-day Ginza shopping, choose one building: Ginza Mitsukoshi for food gifts, or Itoya for stationery. A single purchase mission near the 4-chome crossing is easier to finish than a district-wide shopping list.

Food gifts at Mitsukoshi, or stationery at Itoya

At Ginza Mitsukoshi, use the B2/B3 food floors for packaged sweets or food, rather than touring the fashion departments first. Decide whether the purchase is for the journey or for home: perishable food, liquids and delicate boxes have different packing needs. Read the storage and expiry label before buying.

If stationery is the purpose, make Ginza Itoya the destination instead and select a notebook, pen or paper item. Do not promise both buildings in a 45-minute stop. Allow the last part of your visit for payment and getting back to street or station level.

The 4-chome crossing is a useful anchor

Mitsukoshi is at 4-6-16 Ginza, beside the 4-chome crossing. Its operator quotes a one-minute approach from Ginza Station’s Ginza 4-chome intersection gate, or two minutes from Higashi-ginza via the underground passage toward Ginza. Platform movement is extra.

For Itoya instead, the operator gives about two minutes from Ginza Exit A13 toward Kyobashi. Its main-store address is 2-7-15 Ginza.

Choose the return entrance for your actual train, not merely a sign saying Ginza. If your airport route begins at Tokyo Station, include the journey there: Ginza’s shopping basements are not Tokyo Station’s departure concourse. Use the store’s accessible-route diagrams when stairs are unsuitable.

Keep the purchase useful, and leave the queue optional

Mitsukoshi’s general retail hours are listed as 10:00–20:00; restaurants and food-floor eat-in counters have their own hours. Building browsing has no general entrance charge. Prices depend on the product, and a food hall is not automatically a place to sit down and eat.

For a small purchase, a tax-free procedure or a long gift-wrapping line may use more time than it saves. Ask about the finish time before joining a queue. If it is raining, remain in the chosen building; if the item is unavailable, buy a simpler alternative rather than crossing Ginza to restart the search.
