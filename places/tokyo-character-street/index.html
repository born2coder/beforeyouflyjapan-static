ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Tokyo Character Street before a Haneda flight

Tokyo Character Street is for a focused hunt for a favorite character, not a fast check of every shop. Most of the familiar street is on the Yaesu side’s B1 public concourse, but some shops in the current directory are on 1F. Use the shop map before choosing your route.

Is this the stop for you?

Suggested time at the place
35–60 minutes — local visit only
Choose it for
Fans who can name one or two shops they want to browse, including families staying close to Tokyo Station.

Choose another stop if: A special-release queue or a shop on another floor would consume the available window.

Choose two stores with a reason to enter

For Studio Ghibli goods, start with Donguri Republic; for model vehicles, choose Tomica/Plarail; for soft toys and character gifts, compare Rilakkuma Store & Sumikko Gurashi Shop. These are specific B1 choices in the operator’s reviewed directory. Give the first store most of the visit and make the second optional.

Pokémon Store and DRAGON BALL STORE TOKYO are listed on 1F in the current directory, so do not wander B1 expecting every name to be there. New shop openings and temporary events can alter the route. Allow the final 10–15 minutes for payment, packing and finding the station route rather than one more shelf.

Outside the JR gates, on the Yaesu side

Tokyo Station First Avenue is outside the ticket gates. Follow Yaesu signs from your arrival platform, leave the paid area and use the B1 map to find the selected shops. A Marunouchi-side arrival or a long Shinkansen platform approach adds walking before this local visit begins.

Remember where your luggage is stored and where your onward transport leaves. Crossing to Marunouchi and returning to Yaesu for airport buses is a station traversal, not a free addition. Do not buy a JR entry ticket merely to reach these public-side stores.

Opening time, purchases and an early-morning alternative

The operator’s general Character Street hours are 10:00–20:30, with individual notices taking priority. Browsing has no general admission ticket, while merchandise prices vary by shop. A release-day queue may require a separate admission system or simply make a short visit impractical.

Before 10:00, choose another open station facility rather than waiting in front of closed shutters. With children, agree on one item or one store first and keep a seated break in the plan. The street is indoors, but that does not make queues or crowded shop entrances effortless with a stroller.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Tokyo Character Street, Tokyo Station First Avenue on the map ↗

Both this stop and the 1F JR EAST Travel Service Center for foreign visitors are outside the JR gates. That counter lists 08:30–20:00. The B1 GRANSTA cloakroom is inside the JR gates: it lists 08:30–21:00 opening, but its same-day baggage note gives 20:30, so plan collection by the earlier limit. Published S/M/L fees are ¥500/¥1,000/¥2,000 per item, based on the sum of the three sides. Accepted bags must total less than 250 cm, have no side longer than 2 m and weigh less than 30 kg. Keep the collection deadline on your receipt; entering the JR paid area just for storage can add an unnecessary gate crossing.
