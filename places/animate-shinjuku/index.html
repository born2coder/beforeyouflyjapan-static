Animate Shinjuku: before your flight

One favorite series, rather than a whole-district hunt.

Quick answer: Allow 210 minutes for HND from Shinjuku, without luggage tasks. Same-area storage adds 35 minutes; collecting bags elsewhere can add more. Stroller or short-walking options, where supported, add 20 minutes. Keep another three hours before your flight for airport procedures.

Three things to notice

  • One favorite series, rather than a whole-district hunt.
  • Compare current Japanese manga and character merchandise.
  • Choose one small keepsake and finish checkout before the stage ends.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

11:00–20:00; Marui Men 7F. JR Shinjuku East Exit is about 9 minutes away; Shinjuku-sanchome E2 is closer.

Events and popular releases can have queues; merchandise availability is not guaranteed.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use the live route via Shinagawa and Keikyu, or Hamamatsucho and the Tokyo Monorail. This 90-minute allowance includes waiting, station access and changes.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Store bags at Shinjuku on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Walk to Animate Shinjuku: 20 minutes.
  2. Browse one favorite series at Animate Shinjuku: 50 minutes.
  3. Return to Shinjuku station area: 20 minutes.
  4. Head to Haneda Airport: 90 minutes.
  5. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 30 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
