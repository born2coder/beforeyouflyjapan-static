ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Animate Shinjuku before a Haneda flight

Animate Shinjuku is a focused anime-goods stop on the 7th floor of Shinjuku Marui Men. It is a single shop, not a whole tower of Animate floors: come with a series or character in mind and keep time for the lift and checkout.

Go straight to Marui Men 7F

Take the building lift or escalators to 7F, then use the shop’s floor guide to locate books, character goods or the event area. A screenshot of the Japanese series title or character is more useful to staff than an uncertain English pronunciation. Browse that category first; use remaining minutes for nearby merchandise rather than restarting the whole shop.

Allow roughly 35–50 minutes for the shop visit, including getting upstairs and paying. Limited fairs can have their own entry conditions, queues or sold-out goods. This suggestion is for ordinary shopping and does not include an event reservation.

Shinjuku-sanchome E2 is the short approach

The official shop page places the store at Shinjuku Marui Men, 5-16-4 Shinjuku. It quotes 2 minutes from Shinjuku-sanchome Exit E2, compared with 9 minutes from JR Shinjuku’s East Exit. These are the operator’s walking estimates; platform and lift time are additional.

If your onward journey uses JR or the west-side airport bus area, you still have to return there after shopping. Do not mistake E2 for a shortcut directly to those departure points. Keep the address saved so you do not head to another Marui building.

Buy what fits in the bag you already have

Published shop hours are 11:00–20:00. Ordinary entry is free; merchandise and any separately ticketed events cost extra. Stock is not guaranteed, even when a product appears in promotional material.

Flat goods, books and small accessories are easier last-day purchases than a large boxed figure. Compare the price and condition, then leave room to pack without blocking an aisle. If your chosen series has little stock, avoid automatically adding Ikebukuro: that is a different journey. A short visit to nearby Hanazono Shrine is an alternative only if your remaining route still allows it.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back
