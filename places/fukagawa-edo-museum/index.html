Fukagawa Edo Museum with children before a flight

An indoor Edo street scene with time to pause as a family.

Does it fit?One compact visitAccess & bagsUse my flight time

Quick answer: Allow 335 minutes for HND from Tokyo Station, without luggage tasks. Same-area storage adds 35 minutes; collecting bags elsewhere can add more. Stroller or short-walking options, where supported, add 20 minutes. Keep another three hours before your flight for airport procedures.

YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Explore the recreated Edo streets at Fukagawa Edo Museum, choosing a few houses and shopfronts to discuss with your child. Keep the separate toilet and rest stage in your plan.

What to budget

Paid museum admission; current ticket categories are on the official site.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station.

Keep the museum as the only attraction. Use the museum's barrier-free map for the entrance and lifts rather than adding Kiyosumi Garden to the same visit.

Open Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station on the map ↗

Tokyo Station has a GRANSTA cloakroom on B1 inside the JR gates and a visitor service counter on 1F outside the gates. The published S/M/L fees are ¥500/¥1,000/¥2,000 per item. The B1 counter lists 08:30–21:00 and the outside-gate counter 08:30–20:00. Choose the side you can access and collect before closing; the operator's page is dated 23 May 2025.

Three things to notice

  • An indoor Edo street scene with time to pause as a family.
  • Compare household objects and recreated town lanes.
  • Watch the lighting and sound suggest a changing day.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

Exhibition 9:30–17:00; last admission 16:30. Closed second / fourth Mondays except national holidays, and 29 December–3 January; 2–3 January have special exhibition opening. Holiday exceptions are verified through 2027.

Store bags at Tokyo Station before leaving and return there to collect them. Nursing is available on 1F with staff assistance; museum lift access is confirmed, but the complete station route is not certified step-free.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use the live route via Shinagawa and Keikyu, or Hamamatsucho and the Tokyo Monorail. This 90-minute allowance includes waiting, station access and changes.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Store bags at Tokyo Station on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Travel from Tokyo Station to Fukagawa Edo Museum: 60 minutes.
  2. Visit the recreated Edo neighborhood: 65 minutes.
  3. Use nursing / diaper facilities and rest: 30 minutes.
  4. Return to Tokyo Station station area: 60 minutes.
  5. Head to Haneda Airport: 90 minutes.
  6. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 30 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
