YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Walk the south approach to the main sanctuary, pause at the torii and return the same way. Save the museum and inner garden for a day without a flight deadline.

What to budget

The main shrine grounds are free; the inner garden and museum are separate paid visits. Grounds hours vary by month.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Harajuku Station and Meiji Jingu south entrance.

Use the south entrance near Harajuku for an out-and-back visit. The woodland approach is part of the walking budget, not instant access to the sanctuary.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A Shinto shrine and forest beside Harajuku, approached along a broad gravel path.

What to notice: Notice how quickly the sound of Harajuku fades after the first torii, and pause before each gate rather than rushing straight to the main hall.

Highlights

  • The huge wooden torii gates
  • The shift from city noise to forest quiet
  • The broad approach to the main sanctuary
