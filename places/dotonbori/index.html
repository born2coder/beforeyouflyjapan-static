YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

See the Glico sign from Ebisubashi and choose one short riverwalk section. Pick a takoyaki stall with a short queue and skip the river cruise on this compact route.

What to budget

The riverwalk is free; food, cruises and purchases are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Namba Station and Ebisubashi Bridge.

Keep Nankai Namba as the airport return station. The Dotonbori riverwalk is not beside the KIX train platform.
