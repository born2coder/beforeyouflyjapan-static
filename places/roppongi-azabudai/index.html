YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

For the compact option choose one public space or café at Azabudai Hills. A museum or observation ticket needs a confirmed time slot and its own budget; do not assume a public viewing floor is freely available.

What to budget

Public-area browsing differs from paid exhibitions and viewing facilities; check the chosen facility.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Roppongi Station or Kamiyacho Station.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A contemporary central-Tokyo area of art, architecture, dining and large mixed-use developments.

What to notice: Notice how gardens and pedestrian routes are woven through very large buildings—the experience is the attempt to create a new kind of Tokyo neighborhood.

Highlights

  • Contemporary architecture
  • Public art and landscaped spaces
  • A cosmopolitan evening atmosphere
