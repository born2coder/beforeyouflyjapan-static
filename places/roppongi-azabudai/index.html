ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Roppongi or Azabudai before a Haneda flight

Choose Azabudai Hills or Roppongi Hills before travelling: they are separate places, not one station-side complex. For the compact option, use Azabudai’s Central Green and Market, keeping ticketed art and viewing facilities for a separately timed visit.

Azabudai: Central Green and the Market

From the signed public routes, make your way toward the Central Green for the garden-and-tower setting. Then use Azabudai Hills Market in Garden Plaza C, 1F/B1 for a small food purchase or browse. This gives the visit two named points without trying to tour every tower.

Allow roughly half the local visit for the public outdoor spaces and half for the market and finding your way back. If rain is heavy, make the open market the main activity. Food retailers and restaurants have different service styles: buying from a counter does not necessarily include somewhere to sit and eat.

Kamiyacho is not Roppongi Station

Azabudai Hills is directly connected to Kamiyacho Exit 5 and Roppongi-itchome Exit 4. Its access page gives a ten-minute walk from the listed Roppongi exits. The similar place names hide a real difference in walking distance and platform connections.

Save the station and exit used to arrive, and follow current building signs on the way back. If your intended destination is Roppongi Hills, choose that district instead: do not first travel to Azabudai and then walk over as an unplanned extra. A short local walk should not turn into two sets of station navigation.

No assumed free observation deck

The market’s reviewed general hours are 10:00–20:00, with exceptions by shop. Ordinary public-area browsing is different from admission to teamLab Borderless, an exhibition or a viewing facility. Those need their own ticket, entry conditions and time; this compact visit includes none of them.

Do not head up a tower expecting an unrestricted free observation floor. Choose the actual facility and current access terms first. If a reserved attraction is the reason for your visit, build the rest of the day around that booking rather than adding it after the garden. For a tired traveller, one seated café break in the chosen district may be more useful than another public-space loop.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back
