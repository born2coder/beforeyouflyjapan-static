ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Ginza dinner before a late Haneda flight

For an evening in Ginza before Haneda, make dinner the main event. Ginza Mitsukoshi’s restaurant floors give you several cuisines in one building; a short look at the 4-chome crossing can come before or after, depending on the queue.

Dinner first, with the street view as the flexible part

Begin at the Ginza 4-chome crossing for the illuminated department-store streetscape, then go to Ginza Mitsukoshi’s 11F/12F restaurants. The floor guide lists options including soba, tonkatsu, sushi and Japanese cuisine. Look at the menu and queue before sitting down rather than choosing solely from a photograph.

Our 75–100 minute local allowance is for one meal, movement through the building and a brief street pause. It is not a reservation or a promise about service speed. Tell the restaurant your latest departure time before ordering. A course meal whose finish time is uncertain is a poor match.

Choose Ginza or Higashi-ginza for the next journey

Mitsukoshi is beside the 4-chome crossing at 4-6-16 Ginza. Its official access guide links Ginza Station and Higashi-ginza through the underground passage. Higashi-ginza’s Toei Asakusa Line is a different service from the Tokyo Metro lines at Ginza, so keep the onward train’s operator and destination clear.

Return to street or station level before your transport deadline; the lift ride and the passage are part of leaving dinner. Adding a second shopping building after eating makes that exit less predictable, particularly when everyone is leaving at once.

No table? Change the meal, not the airport deadline

The department store’s 20:00 retail closing does not establish the restaurant’s last-order time. Use the individual restaurant listing for dinner hours and prices. Your bill depends on the menu, drinks and any displayed service or cover conditions; no meal is included in the planner.

If the queue is long, choose another restaurant on the same floors with immediate seating. A food-floor purchase is a fallback only while that floor is open and when you have somewhere suitable to eat it. Otherwise, move toward Haneda and make dinner an airport meal. The outside street view can be shortened to a few minutes without spoiling the evening.
