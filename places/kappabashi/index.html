YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Choose one category, such as bowls, chopsticks or food replicas. Use the official store directory to pick two nearby shops before arriving, and leave packing time for breakable items.

What to budget

Street browsing is free. Shop hours vary; the association says only about 30% of stores open on Sundays.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Tawaramachi Station and Kappabashi Dougu Street.

Use the street association's access map. The Asakusa starting area in the planner includes an approach allowance; it is not the same as already standing in a shop.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A specialist shopping street for cookware, knives, tableware and replica food.

What to notice: Look at how each shop specializes in one professional craft; even without buying, the precision reveals the unseen work behind a Japanese meal.

Highlights

  • Japanese knives and cookware
  • Ceramics and lacquerware
  • Replica food samples
