ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Kappabashi kitchenware shopping before Haneda

Kappabashi is a useful last stop when you want a small kitchen souvenir with a clear purpose. Choose tableware and chopsticks in the southern section, or make food replicas your destination farther along the street; those are different walks.

Is this the stop for you?

Suggested time at the place
60–90 minutes — local visit only
Choose it for
A practical kitchen gift, chopsticks or a small food-replica souvenir.

Choose another stop if: Your target shop is closed, or fragile purchases and engraving would make packing rushed.

Two concrete shopping options

For tableware, begin at Niimi, the south-entrance shop with the giant chef figure, then consider Hashitou Honten for wooden or lacquered chopsticks. A small bowl, chopstick rest or one pair of chopsticks is easier to pack than a large serving dish. Ask how fragile items will be wrapped before paying.

For a food-replica gift, make Ganso Shokuhin Sample-ya’s Kappabashi shop the main destination instead. Its magnets and small replica goods can be a complete purchase mission. A wax-making workshop is a separately reserved activity; it is not included in a short shopping allowance.

The nearest station depends on which shop you choose

The street association gives a five-minute general approach from Tawaramachi. That describes reaching the district, not every shop. Ganso’s own map quotes 12 minutes from Tawaramachi Exit 3, or 5 minutes from Tsukuba Express Asakusa Exit A2. The Tobu and Toei Asakusa stations are different locations.

For the southern tableware route, use Niimi at 1-1-1 Matsugaya as the starting landmark. For Ganso, save 3-7-6 Nishi-Asakusa. Do not walk to one end of the street and then discover that the intended store is at the other.

Sundays change the choice

The association says only about 30% of shops open on Sundays. Niimi and Hashitou list Sunday/public-holiday closures, whereas Ganso lists Sunday and holiday shopping hours of 10:00–17:30, with year-end closures. Choose the actual shop before making a Sunday journey.

Browsing has no street admission charge; products and workshops are extra. Kitchen knives require appropriate packing and airline-compliant checked baggage, so they are awkward if baggage is already checked or you travel cabin-bag only. If packing or a service queue takes too long, choose a small ready-to-carry gift and leave rather than adding another specialist store.
