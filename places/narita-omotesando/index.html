YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Choose a short stretch of Narita Omotesando and one unagi restaurant with immediate seating. If lunch has a long queue, keep the street walk and eat at the airport.

What to budget

Street browsing is free; unagi meals vary by restaurant and are not included in the planner.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: JR Narita or Keisei Narita Station.

Choose JR or Keisei according to your airport train. The Omotesando approach slopes toward Naritasan, so leave energy and time for the walk back.

Open JR Narita or Keisei Narita Station on the map ↗

Narita's tourism association identifies lockers in the JR Narita station passage, by JR Narita's Central/Omotesando exit and by Keisei Narita's Central/Omotesando exit. Choose the operator used by your airport train. Local fees have not been verified; for oversized bags, confirm a suitable locker before walking downhill or keep the bag at your hotel or airport instead.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A sloping temple approach of restaurants, sweets, crafts and traditional shopfronts.

What to notice: Notice the eel restaurants preparing food near the street and the way the road gradually reveals the temple district below.

Highlights

  • Sloping historic approach
  • Eel restaurants and traditional food
  • Small gifts and local sweets
