ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Tokyo Station and Marunouchi before a flight

For a short Tokyo Station visit, the Marunouchi side is the red-brick facade and open square; Yaesu is a different side with many shops and airport buses. A relaxed exterior walk works well when you choose which side your return journey needs.

Choose it for
A free architecture-and-city-view stop near Tokyo Station, with an optional indoor pause.

Choose another stop if: Your baggage and onward transport are on the opposite side and a station crossing would make the stop rushed.

The station square, then one view from KITTE

Leave toward the Marunouchi square and step far enough back to see the length of the red-brick building rather than standing in a doorway. Take a short walk across the public square and choose whether to continue to KITTE, beside the station’s south end, or return directly.

KITTE Garden on 6F offers a view back over the station when open. Keep it as the second part of one compact visit, with time for lifts and finding the exit. Do not combine it automatically with Character Street on the far side of the station. The square and garden have no general admission charge.

Pick a Marunouchi exit and keep the return side visible

Use the station’s Marunouchi North, Central or South exit according to your chosen meeting point. For KITTE, the south side is the useful landmark. Tokyo Station’s paid concourse, public passages and department-store floors are different routes; a shop name is not a substitute for a saved station exit.

If your airport bus leaves from Yaesu, include the full crossing and the booked boarding point after this walk. If using a rail service, preserve time to reach the actual platform from the public square. A station-side stop can still be a long way from a particular train.

Weather and hours change the value of the garden

KITTE lists its 6F garden 11:00–23:00 on weekdays and 11:00–22:00 on weekends/holidays, with weather closures. Before 11:00, the station facade itself remains the appropriate purpose; do not promise an early roof-garden visit.

In rain, replace the garden with an open indoor café or shop, keeping the station side consistent. A seated meal needs extra time beyond our short exterior-and-view allowance. Skip the second viewpoint if the lift queue or weather makes it less pleasant; the station square is already a complete small experience.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Tokyo Station, Marunouchi side on the map ↗

Both this stop and the 1F JR EAST Travel Service Center for foreign visitors are outside the JR gates. That counter lists 08:30–20:00. The B1 GRANSTA cloakroom is inside the JR gates: it lists 08:30–21:00 opening, but its same-day baggage note gives 20:30, so plan collection by the earlier limit. Published S/M/L fees are ¥500/¥1,000/¥2,000 per item, based on the sum of the three sides. Accepted bags must total less than 250 cm, have no side longer than 2 m and weigh less than 30 kg. Keep the collection deadline on your receipt; entering the JR paid area just for storage can add an unnecessary gate crossing.
