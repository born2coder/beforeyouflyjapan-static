YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

See the red-brick facade from the Marunouchi station square, then choose one souvenir zone. Keep Tokyo Character Street on the Yaesu side as an alternative, not an automatic extra.

What to budget

The station-square walk is free; paid-gate shops require the appropriate access and purchases are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Tokyo Station, Marunouchi side.

The red-brick facade is on the Marunouchi side. Many airport buses use the Yaesu side; allow a separate station crossing if that is your booked route.

Open Tokyo Station, Marunouchi side on the map ↗

Tokyo Station has a GRANSTA cloakroom on B1 inside the JR gates and a visitor service counter on 1F outside the gates. The published S/M/L fees are ¥500/¥1,000/¥2,000 per item. The B1 counter lists 08:30–21:00 and the outside-gate counter 08:30–20:00. Choose the side you can access and collect before closing; the operator's page is dated 23 May 2025.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

Tokyo’s principal intercity station and the business district facing its restored Marunouchi building.

What to notice: View the facade from the plaza before going inside, then look for regional food and gifts that bring the rest of Japan into one station.

Highlights

  • The Marunouchi red-brick facade
  • Station bento and regional souvenirs
  • The old-station and modern-skyline contrast
