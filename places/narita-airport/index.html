Narita Airport: what to do before your flight

At Narita, choose a meal, a useful souvenir or an aircraft view in your own terminal. A short flight-free window is easily lost by changing buildings. First decide whether you are still before security or have already entered the international departure area.

Choose another stop if: You would need to change terminals for the only shop you want, or procedures still require immediate attention.

A small public-side plan in Terminal1 or2

In T1, a stationery browse at TRAVELER’S FACTORY AIRPORT on 4F can be a compact souvenir stop; the airport directory lists it before security. For an aircraft view, T1’s observation deck is on 5F. In T2, use the 4F observation-deck areas instead of travelling to T1 for the same activity.

The deck listing gives 07:00–21:00 from October through March, and 06:30–21:00 from April through September, with weather restrictions. A 20–30 minute view or 45–60 minute meal is our local suggestion; it does not include collecting bags or airline procedures. The decks have no general sightseeing admission ticket.

Terminal3 is beyond the Terminal2 railway stop

Narita Airport Terminal 1 station serves T1; Airport Terminal 2 station is the rail stop for both T2 and T3. For T3, the airport quotes about 13 minutes from the station gates to the T3 information counter using the access corridor. Getting to your airline and completing procedures take additional time.

The free terminal buses are another option, but their ride times do not include the wait. If meeting someone, name the terminal, floor and public-side landmark. “At Narita” is too broad to make a reliable meeting point.

Do not choose an airside shop while waiting landside

The airport shop search labels each outlet Before Security Check or After Security Check. T1’s Narita Nakamise is an international airside shopping area; it is not a public mall you can browse while waiting for bag drop. A boarding pass and completion of the required procedures determine access.

If weather closes the deck, stay with an indoor shop or meal in the correct building. Do not return to Narita town merely because you dislike waiting: the airport-to-town ride creates fresh walking, waiting and baggage stages that this short airport visit does not include.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Narita International Airport on the map ↗

Narita's storage directory lists counters by terminal and floor, with prices set by each provider and bag size. For example, JAL ABC departure-floor counters are listed at T1 4F and T2 3F, 07:00–21:00. Arrival-floor counters can have different hours. Collect before security and allow time to reach your actual departure terminal; T3 passengers should not treat a T2 collection stop as being in the same building.
