YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Use ecute Shinagawa for one food purchase if you already have access to the JR paid area. Otherwise choose a station-side meal outside the gates; do not buy a rail ticket just to reach a food shop.

What to budget

Food and purchases are extra. JR-gated shops may require a valid ticket or admission, unlike an outside-gate café.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Shinagawa Station, JR and Keikyu concourse.

Choose your meal on the side of the gates you can legally access. ecute Shinagawa is inside JR gates; the Keikyu airport route has its own gate and platform.

Open Shinagawa Station, JR and Keikyu concourse on the map ↗

One documented option is the Multi ecube locker bank inside the JR gates, on ecute Shinagawa's south passage near the stairs to platforms 7 and 8. Its S/M/L/LW usage charges are ¥500/¥700/¥900/¥1,400; online reservation adds ¥500, making the displayed reservation totals ¥1,000/¥1,200/¥1,400/¥1,900. This is not an outside-gate Keikyu locker. Confirm access and availability before storing.
