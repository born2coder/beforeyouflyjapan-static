YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

At Ueno Toshogu, focus on the carved gate, gold decoration and lantern approach. The paid precinct is distinct from entering the shrine buildings, which are not open for a casual interior visit.

What to budget

The inner precinct requires admission; the peony garden and special events are separate. Check current ticket categories.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Ueno Station and Ueno Park.

Use the shrine's current access map through Ueno Park. Keep the return to your chosen Ueno station in the timeline instead of walking toward another museum.

Three things to notice

  • Golden architecture against the green of Ueno Park.
  • Compare the carved gate with the stone lantern approach.
  • Look for fine details in the gilded sanctuary exterior; this is an exterior visit.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

Paid precinct: 9:30–16:00 October–February; 9:30–17:00 March–September. Adult admission currently ¥700; grounds and paid precinct have different hours.

Weather or ceremonies can close the paid area. Park paths add walking; this route is not offered as a low-walking option.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use the live route via Shinagawa and Keikyu, or Hamamatsucho and the Tokyo Monorail. This 90-minute allowance includes waiting, station access and changes.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Store bags at Ueno on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Walk to Ueno Toshogu: 25 minutes.
  2. Visit Ueno Toshogu paid inner precinct: 45 minutes.
  3. Return to Ueno station area: 20 minutes.
  4. Head to Haneda Airport: 90 minutes.
  5. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 30 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
