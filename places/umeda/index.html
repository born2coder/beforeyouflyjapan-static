YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Choose Osaka Station City for one seated meal or a short public-space pause. Keep an observation attraction as an alternative only when a booked slot and return walk fit.

What to budget

Public-area browsing is free; dining, shopping and paid observation facilities are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: JR Osaka Station or your booked airport bus stop.

Choose the JR airport-train platform or exact airport-bus stop before shopping. Osaka, Osaka-Umeda and Umeda are several connected stations, not one departure gate.

Open JR Osaka Station or your booked airport bus stop on the map ↗

Osaka's tourism-board map lists Crosta Osaka at JR Osaka Station, 09:00–20:00, at ¥1,000 per item for temporary storage. The listing is dated 1 October 2025; check the operator's current conditions and keep collection before closing. Choose the counter closest to your actual airport train or bus departure.
