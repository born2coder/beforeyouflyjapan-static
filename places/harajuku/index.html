YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Pick one shop on Takeshita Street and one short street pass. When the crowd stops moving freely, turn back rather than trying to reach every shop.

What to budget

Street entry is free; shopping and cafés are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Harajuku Station, Takeshita Street side.

Use Takeshita Street as the turnaround point rather than continuing to Shibuya. Photograph the station entrance you intend to return to.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A compact fashion and youth-culture district between Harajuku Station, Takeshita Street and Omotesando.

What to notice: Look beyond the crowds: window displays, handmade accessories and the change in style as you move toward Omotesando tell the story of contemporary Tokyo.

Highlights

  • Takeshita Street’s concentrated energy
  • Small fashion and character shops
  • The contrast with nearby Omotesando
