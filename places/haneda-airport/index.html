ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Haneda Airport: food, shops and views before departure

At Haneda, the easiest last experience is in the terminal your flight actually uses. For a Terminal 3 departure, the public-side Edo Ko-ji and aircraft-viewing deck make a compact visit without another train journey. They are not facilities you can visit after entering the international gate area.

Terminal 3: a small Edo street or a runway view

From the 3F departure lobby, choose one direction: go to 4F Edo Ko-ji for food and traditional shopfronts, or continue toward 5F for TIAT Sky Road and the observation deck. Do not make all three a checklist. Our local allowance is about 20–30 minutes for the deck, or 45–60 minutes for an unhurried meal if seating is available.

At Haneda Nihonbashi, look at the timber bridge and the Edo scenes around it rather than treating the space as a shortcut to another terminal. The deck is free to enter; food and purchases are extra. Neither a café queue nor an outdoor view deserves the time reserved for security.

T2 and T3 are different destinations

International flights can use T2 or T3. For T3, leave the train at Haneda Airport Terminal 3 and follow Departures to 3F. If using T2, choose its own public facilities: the outdoor observation deck is on 5F, and the indoor FLIGHT DECK TOKYO offers a weather-sheltered alternative.

This suggestion stays on the public side. Once airside, use shops near your gate instead. A terminal shuttle, lift journey and repeat navigation turn a casual cross-terminal detour into a separate outing.

Late arrival, bad weather or a closed restaurant?

The official deck listing gives T1/T2 hours of 06:30–22:00 and T3 access around the clock, with possible weather or security closures. A 24-hour terminal does not mean every restaurant is open. Use the restaurant directory for your terminal and the public/airside area shown there.

If the deck closes, choose the nearby indoor public area or proceed to the airline procedures. A lounge is not a general waiting room: eligible cards, airline conditions and the location beyond security determine whether you can enter.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Haneda Airport, Tokyo on the map ↗

Use a locker in your own terminal, outside security. Haneda lists T1/T2 prices of ¥400/¥500/¥600/¥1,000 per 24 hours, with ¥1,500 XXL compartments at selected banks. T3 is different: S/M/L cost ¥500/¥700/¥900; the 3F south bank also lists ¥2,000 XXL compartments. Storage is limited to seven days. Collect your bag before entering security; a locker in another terminal adds a transfer and a second collection stop.
