YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Make one pass through Ameyoko and choose a no-queue snack or meal. A museum in Ueno Park is a separate admission and closing-time commitment, not a free extra.

What to budget

Market browsing is free; meals, purchases and museum tickets are extra.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: JR Ueno or Keisei-Ueno Station.

Choose JR Ueno or Keisei-Ueno before storing bags. The Ameyoko shopping streets sit between Ueno and Okachimachi, not inside the Skyliner terminal.

Open JR Ueno or Keisei-Ueno Station on the map ↗

If your airport route uses the Skyliner, the Keisei-Ueno baggage counter avoids a return to JR Ueno. Keisei Travel lists ¥600/¥1,000/¥1,500 for S/M/L and ¥2,000 for oversized items. These are height-based categories; confirm counter hours and collect before your train.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A broad cultural park beside a lively railway-side market district.

What to notice: Pay attention to the transition from the open park to the railway-side market—the abrupt change in sound, scale and pace is the heart of this stop.

Highlights

  • Ueno’s broad park setting
  • Ameyoko’s market lanes
  • Street food and everyday shopping
