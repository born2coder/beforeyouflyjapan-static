YOUR LAST STOP, IN PRACTICE

One visit worth making

Walk to Zojoji's main approach and look back toward Tokyo Tower. For the short route, enjoy the exterior view and return; do not add a deck ticket on arrival.

What to budget

Exterior sightseeing does not include paid temple exhibitions or Tokyo Tower admission.

Find the entrance and keep bags on the return route

Route anchor: Hamamatsucho Station and Daimon.

Keep Hamamatsucho's Tokyo Monorail entrance as your return anchor. Daimon subway and JR entrances do not lead directly to the monorail platform.

Open Hamamatsucho Station and Daimon on the map ↗

Keep collection on the Hamamatsucho return route. Before walking to Hamamatsucho Station and Daimon, confirm a locker or staffed counter, its closing time and the exact gate side. Local storage prices have not been verified for this stop; use the posted rate rather than assuming the same price as another station. If no suitable space is available, keep the bag and choose a station meal or the airport instead.

WHY GO

Why this place is worth your time

A major Buddhist temple and gateway district on the Haneda side of central Tokyo.

What to notice: Walk far enough into the grounds to turn back toward the main hall and tower—the changing alignment is the experience, not just either landmark alone.

Highlights

  • Zojoji’s main gate and hall
  • Tokyo Tower rising behind the temple
  • Quiet temple details close to the business city
