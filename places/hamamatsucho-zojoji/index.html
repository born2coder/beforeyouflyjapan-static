ONE LAST JAPAN STOP

Zojoji and Hamamatsucho before Haneda

Zojoji offers a temple courtyard with Tokyo Tower rising behind it, while keeping Hamamatsucho as a clear return point for the Tokyo Monorail. Choose the temple exterior and main approach; going up Tokyo Tower is a separate visit.

A temple visit with the tower as the backdrop

Walk the main approach toward Zojoji’s main hall, then pause where the hall and Tokyo Tower share the view. The contrast between the temple roof and the red-and-white tower is the point of this compact stop; you do not have to climb the tower to enjoy it.

Allow roughly 20–30 minutes within the temple area, plus the walk out and back. The Sangedatsumon gate is under conservation work in the current official tourism notice, so do not expect an unobstructed gate photograph. Follow the temporary approach signs and keep out of repair areas rather than trying to recover an older postcard view.

Hamamatsucho is the return anchor

Zojoji’s operator lists about 10 minutes on foot from JR/Tokyo Monorail Hamamatsucho and 5 minutes from Daimon. Use the official map for the approach through the Daimon area toward the temple, then return along the route you recognize.

A nearby Daimon subway entrance does not take you straight onto the monorail platform. If the airport journey uses Tokyo Monorail, allow the walk back and movement to its own entrance. With a stroller, use the operator’s station layout to choose lifts; a ten-minute general walking estimate does not certify an accessible route.

Keep paid exhibits and a tower ascent separate

The ordinary exterior visit is different from admission to the Zojoji Treasures Gallery or the Tokugawa mausoleum. Each has its own admission and opening conditions. Pay for one only when the time remaining genuinely covers it; do not discover the extra visit length after buying.

In heavy rain, the outdoor photograph may not be worth a wet return with luggage. A seated meal near Hamamatsucho keeps the departure route simpler. If the temple walk is still appealing, shorten it to the main courtyard and return without adding the tower’s ticket line.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Hamamatsucho Station and Daimon on the map ↗

JR East confirms coin lockers at Hamamatsucho Station. If the station is on your airport route, storing there can keep collection beside the Monorail connection instead of adding a separate neighbourhood return. Use the station map for the bank you can reach, and keep the key or receipt until collection. The JR facility listing does not establish current bank prices or bag dimensions, so these are not quoted as verified fees.
