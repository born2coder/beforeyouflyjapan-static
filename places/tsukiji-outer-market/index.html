Tsukiji Outer Market: Hours, Map & a Short Food Walk

Tsukiji Outer Market is for a food walk among specialist shops, not a visit to Toyosu’s wholesale auction. Pick one thing you want to eat, one lane to browse and the station you will return to. There is no single opening time for every stall.

Is this the stop for you?

Suggested time at the place
60–90 minutes — local visit only
Choose it for
A morning or early-afternoon food stop with a flexible choice of open stalls.

Choose another stop if: A closure day leaves too few suitable shops, the lanes are too crowded with your luggage, or it is already late afternoon.

One meal and one lane is enough

For a named tamagoyaki stop, Tsukiji Yamacho at 4-10-10 Tsukiji is listed by the market association as open 06:00–15:30 with no regular closing day. It is one example, not a reason to wait through a long queue. Start with your main food choice rather than sampling every queue. For a simple route, use Tsukiji Uogashi and the surrounding outer-market lanes as identifiable anchors, then browse for tea, dried bonito, tamagoyaki or a small kitchen item. The market directory groups shops by these specialties, making a chosen interest easier to follow than a list of famous counters.

Give around 45–60 minutes to eating and nearby browsing, then keep 15–30 for navigation, a pause and the return. These are our local allowances. If the first queue will consume them, move to another open stall or a seated meal elsewhere. Follow the shop’s eating and rubbish rules rather than treating the narrow lanes as an unrestricted picnic space.

Choose Tsukiji or Tsukijishijo for the return you need

The market association lists approximately one-minute approaches from Tsukiji on the Hibiya Line and Tsukijishijo on the Oedo Line, and about three minutes from Higashi-ginza. Those are approaches to the district, not walking times from every platform to a chosen stall.

Use the market map to locate your return station and Plat Tsukiji information centre before getting absorbed in the shops. Tsukiji, Tsukijishijo and Toyosu are different destinations. Do not append an auction trip simply because a guide calls this the fish-market area.

A useful visiting window and a concrete help point

We suggest approximately 09:00–14:00 for this short food visit, subject to the official calendar and each shop. Some businesses open earlier, while closure days, holidays and late afternoons can offer far less choice. An accessible street is not proof of an open kitchen.

Plat Tsukiji offers maps, English-language help and coin lockers of several sizes. Its reviewed English listing gives the counter 08:00–14:00 on weekdays/Saturdays and 10:00–14:00 on Sundays, holidays and closure days, with year-end closures. Use that as a help point, not a promise of an empty locker or evening collection access. Street browsing is free; budget from the menu you actually select.

Leave bags where you can collect them on the way back

Find Tsukiji or Tsukijishijo Station on the map ↗

PLAT TSUKIJI has small, medium and large coin lockers in two locations in the building. This keeps a market visit closer to your bag than a separate Tokyo Station drop-off. The official facility page does not publish locker prices or collection hours. Collect as you leave the market; for an evening pickup, use another storage option with a stated deadline rather than assuming the information desk or lounge hours also cover the lockers.
