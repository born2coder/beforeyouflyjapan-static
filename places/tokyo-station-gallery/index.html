Tokyo Station Gallery family art stop: before your flight

Art and Tokyo Station’s historic brickwork in one visit.

Quick answer: Allow 240 minutes for HND from Tokyo Station, without luggage tasks. Same-area storage adds 35 minutes; collecting bags elsewhere can add more. Stroller or short-walking options, where supported, add 20 minutes. Keep another three hours before your flight for airport procedures.

Three things to notice

  • Art and Tokyo Station’s historic brickwork in one visit.
  • Look at the exposed building fabric alongside the exhibition.
  • Choose a small number of works to discuss with your child.

Opening hours, tickets and exclusions

10:00–18:00, Fridays to 20:00; last admission 30 minutes before closing. The planner conservatively finishes by 18:00. Verified exhibition calendar: 19 September–8 November 2026; Mondays closed except 21 September, 12 October and 2 November. 13 October closed.

This candidate is disabled outside the verified exhibition dates until its calendar is refreshed. Exhibition admission is required for the interior history / brick experience.

Airport and luggage route

Check live trains and your airline terminal before leaving. Use the live route via Shinagawa and Keikyu, or Hamamatsucho and the Tokyo Monorail. This 90-minute allowance includes waiting, station access and changes.

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Store bags at Tokyo Station on the airport return route. Locker space is not guaranteed. Photograph the exact entrance, floor and receipt; collection has its own stage in the planner.

A compact visit

  1. Walk to Tokyo Station Gallery family art stop: 15 minutes.
  2. Visit Tokyo Station Gallery: 60 minutes.
  3. Use toilets, diaper facilities and a seated break: 30 minutes.
  4. Return to Tokyo Station station area: 15 minutes.
  5. Head to Haneda Airport: 90 minutes.
  6. Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin: 30 minutes.

These are planning allowances, not a train timetable. If delayed, cut optional stages and protect airport time.
