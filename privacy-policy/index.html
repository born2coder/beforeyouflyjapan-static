Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Before You Fly Japan publishes this website and handles enquiries through its contact form. This policy explains the information used by the planner, hosting, measurement and contact services.

Planner conditions in your browser

The route calculation runs in your browser. We do not create a traveller account or save your entered flight and free-time conditions to a traveller profile. The browser tab uses session storage to remember the airport, dates and times, starting area, luggage choice, interests and walking preference while you revise a plan. Closing the tab normally ends that storage session; browser restore features may preserve it.

Plan links and hosting requests

A personalised plan address contains conditions such as the flight time, starting area and luggage choice. Anyone receiving that address can read those conditions. URLs can also remain in browser history, and the hosting provider receives the requested URL when serving a page. Do not put booking references, passport information, payment details or health information in the planner or a shared address.

Google Analytics and site measurement

We use Google Analytics to understand pages viewed and actions such as starting or completing the planner and selecting a plan. Our Google tag configuration supplies page addresses and referrers without their query strings or fragments. Our custom planner events use limited fields such as the airport, starting area, available minutes, luggage category and route identifier; they do not include the entered flight or free-time timestamps.

Google may process device, browser, approximate location, identifiers and interaction information under its own policies. Analytics may use cookies. Cloudflare supplies hosting, traffic security and site-performance measurement and may process IP addresses, request information, browser details and similar technical data. URL sanitisation for our Google tag does not remove the URL from your browser, shared links or hosting requests.

See Google's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy for their handling of data.

Advertising and Google cookies

Editorial guides may carry Google-provided advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and other identifiers to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve personalised ads based on those visits.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center, and review participating vendors' choices through YourAdChoices. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising. Browser privacy settings can block or delete cookies and restrict storage; that may affect website functions.

Contact enquiries

The contact form processes the name, reply email, enquiry type and message you provide. Cloudflare Turnstile supplies a security check, and our server uses rate limits and validation to reduce abuse. Resend delivers the notification email to the site's enquiry inbox. We use the submitted contact information to handle the enquiry and do not publish it on the website.

Please avoid sensitive documents or private booking details. You may request correction or deletion of an enquiry through the same form. Identification of the relevant message may be needed before it can be located.

Questions and changes

For a privacy question, contact Before You Fly Japan through the enquiry form. We update this page when our services or data practices materially change.
