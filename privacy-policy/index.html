Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy explains how Before You Fly Japan handles information when you use this website.

Planner information

The planner runs in your browser. We do not create a traveller account or save the flight details, free-time values and preferences you enter to a Before You Fly Japan user profile. Your browser may temporarily retain ordinary technical data needed to display and operate the site.

Analytics, cookies and similar technologies

We may use analytics and similar technologies to understand aggregate activity, such as planner completion, plan selection, pages viewed and cases where no plan is found. These services may process device, browser, approximate location and interaction information under their own terms. Where required by law, we will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies.

Advertising and Google cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s previous visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve personalised ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center. Other third-party advertising vendors or networks may also use cookies to serve ads on this site. You can review participating vendors and available opt-out choices at YourAdChoices. These choices do not necessarily stop all advertising; they may cause ads to be less personalised.

Contact enquiries

When you use the contact form, we process the name, reply email, enquiry type and message you submit so we can answer your enquiry and protect the service from abuse. Cloudflare provides security and bot-prevention services, and Resend delivers the notification email. We do not publish the contact details you enter.

Sensitive information

Do not enter booking references, passport details, payment details, health information or other sensitive personal information into the planner or contact form.

Your choices and questions

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functions. For a privacy question or a request relating to information submitted through the contact form, please use our contact page.
