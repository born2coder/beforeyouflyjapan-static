ABOUT BEFORE YOU FLY JAPAN

A useful last day, with a clear way to the airport.

A checkout time and a flight time leave an awkward gap. A whole afternoon can look free until you add the suitcase at the hotel, the walk to the train and the journey to the right terminal. Before You Fly Japan helps turn that gap into one manageable last stop.

Who publishes these guides

Before You Fly Japan is the publisher and editorial identity of this website. The site combines English-language departure-day guides with a free planning tool for Haneda, Narita and Kansai International Airport. Editorial enquiries and corrections are handled through our contact form; choose “Report a correction” to flag a route, price or opening-hour issue.

What you can decide here

Start with your actual airport, flight date, free time, starting area and luggage. The planner compares authored routes and shows when to start, when to leave the final area and when to be ready for airline procedures. Destination guides explain what to see, how to reach a specific stop and when a simpler alternative makes more sense.

A station meal can be the right choice. A long shopping trip can be the wrong one even when the flight is late. Our recommendations are built around the entire route rather than a list of famous places.

How the information is made

We research airport, railway, attraction and local tourism sources, then write a compact visit and assign planning allowances for movement and delays. We use calculation checks and simulated traveller scenarios to review the results. These are desk-researched guides: a source review or a software test is not a claim that an editor walked the route, measured a queue or interviewed travellers on site.

Each guide identifies its sources and separates published facts from suggested visit lengths. Photographs carry their own credits and do not establish that we visited the location. See how we research and calculate a route for the details.

What stays with the operator

Your airline sets check-in, baggage and boarding deadlines. Operators control train departures, admission, storage availability and closures. We do not make bookings, provide live queue measurements or guarantee that a model journey will run to the minute. When circumstances change, the optional stop should become shorter.

Corrections and accommodation teams

A useful correction includes the page address, the detail that has changed and an operator notice or other source when available. Please leave booking references and passport details out of the message. Hotels and hostels can also use our free guest-information cards to introduce the planner at checkout.
