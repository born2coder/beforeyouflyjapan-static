ABOUT BEFORE YOU FLY

Built for the gap between checkout and takeoff.

A checkout time and a flight time often leave travelers with a strange gap: too long to sit at the airport, but too short to plan another full day. Before You Fly is built for that gap.

One more Japan experience

We want the final hours of a trip to feel intentional. A market breakfast, a quiet shrine approach, a focused shopping stop or one last view of Tokyo can still fit—when the route and leave-by time are clear.

What the planner does

We work backwards from your flight using your airport, starting area, luggage and interests. Plans use conservative walking and transport allowances, then show exactly when sightseeing must end.

Enjoy more. Rush less.

Your airline and live operating information always take priority. When a city experience does not fit safely, the planner says so and keeps you closer to the airport.
