DEPARTURE-DAY GUIDE

How Early to Arrive at Narita for an International Flight

Narita’s official guide says to arrive at least 2 hours before an international departure, with timing dependent on the airline’s check-in opening time. Before You Fly uses a more generous 3-hour planning allowance for its sightseeing routes. Airline instructions can require an earlier target.

Choose the rail stop for the terminal, then include the final walk

TerminalWhere you get off and where check-in is
T1Narita Airport Terminal 1 station; international check-in on 4F.
T2Airport Terminal 2 station; international check-in on 3F.
T3Use Airport Terminal 2 station, then the signed corridor or appropriate shuttle. Check-in is on 2F in T3.

The airport quotes about 13 minutes from the ticket gates of Airport Terminal 2 Station to the T3 departure-lobby information counter. It expressly excludes onward movement to check-in and the procedures themselves. It is not “13 minutes from train to gate.”

Two ways the same 21:00 flight can use the afternoon

For a 21:00 international flight, these examples protect an 18:00 terminal-ready target. The components below are deliberately rounded planning allowances, not advertised train running times or departures you can book. They assume you are already at the collection point when sightseeing ends; travel back to a distant hotel is additional.

Example and component orderCalculationEnd sightseeing / begin bag collection
Tokyo Station: pickup 20 + platform/walk 10 + wait 20 + rail allowance 75 + terminal movement 2020 + 10 + 20 + 75 + 20 = 145 min15:35
Keisei-Ueno corridor: pickup 20 + approach 15 + wait 20 + rail allowance 55 + terminal movement 2020 + 15 + 20 + 55 + 20 = 130 min15:50

The example cutoffs are 15:35 and 15:50 respectively. They are comparisons built from stated inputs, not a claim that one operator always saves 15 minutes. For T3, replace the terminal allowance with enough time for its corridor and your counter location.

When to keep the last stop in Narita town—or skip it

If you are already in Narita with bags near the station, a compact Omotesando walk can avoid a trip back to Tokyo. If you are in central Tokyo, do not add town sightseeing after reaching the airport unless a new complete itinerary shows it fits. Airport-to-town travel adds two journeys, storage decisions and waiting.

If your airline asks you to arrive earlier: use that earlier terminal-ready time. The current planner uses a fixed three-hour international allowance and has no field for an earlier airline target. Do not accept a suggested route unless it also fits the earlier requirement; shorten it or go directly to the airport.
