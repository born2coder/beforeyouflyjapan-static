DEPARTURE-DAY GUIDE

Haneda Terminal 3 Departures: Floors, Map & What to Do

At Haneda Terminal 3, go to 3F for international departures. Find your flight’s current check-in counter before choosing food or shopping. The public-side Edo Ko-ji and observation deck are different from shops and lounges beyond security.

Open the official T3 floor guide and use its floor labels rather than a broad airport map pin. If your booking uses T2, this is not the departure lobby for your flight.

From the station, follow Departures—not Arrivals

  1. Get off at Haneda Airport Terminal 3, not the T1/T2 rail stop.
  2. Use the signed route to Departures / 3F. With a case or stroller, follow lifts rather than improvising through another floor.
  3. Read your flight number on the display and locate the counter. Assignments can change by flight and day.
  4. Complete the airline’s baggage and document requirements, then security and departure immigration.
  5. Find the boarding gate and its deadline before deciding how long to spend in a shop or lounge.

For a planning example, reserve 20 minutes for the station-to-counter movement when calculating the city journey. That is our rounded allowance for orientation and lifts, not a measured promise from every platform to every counter. Keikyu’s station-to-terminal drawings show the rail approach.

Choose the extra stop around the next required procedure

Genuinely spare timeA suitable use
15–20 minutesStay near the required procedure for a toilet stop, water or a nearby quick purchase.
30–45 minutesIf still public-side, take a short deck visit or browse, with walking back included.
Around an hour or moreA seated meal can work if the queue, service and next procedure leave room. Ask about a wait before joining it.

These are optional-visit allowances, not permission to arrive 15, 30 or 60 minutes before takeoff. Once airside, choose facilities near the actual gate route; do not plan to return to Edo Ko-ji for dinner.

Two services that are easy to misunderstand

Locker storage: Haneda lists coin lockers outside the gates. Save the locker position and collect the bag before departure procedures. An empty small compartment does not establish that your large case can be stored.

TIAT LOUNGE: this is not an unrestricted public waiting room. Access depends on the lounge’s location, your flight and the accepted airline/card or paid-entry conditions. A card accepted at a domestic POWER LOUNGE is not automatically accepted at TIAT. Use the current official lounge listing before relying on admission; capacity and hours do not guarantee a seat.

If you arrive before your counter opens

Keep the case and essentials on the public side, find an open nearby meal or rest option and return for the airline’s opening time. An open terminal does not mean every counter or restaurant is operating. For a wait through the night, T3’s overnight eligibility rules matter as well as opening hours.
