DEPARTURE-DAY GUIDE

Haneda Terminal 3 Departures: Floors, Map & What to Do

YOUR VISIT, EXPLAINED

International departures at Haneda Terminal 3 start on 3F. Go to your airline’s counter or the required departure procedure first. Edo Ko-ji and the observation deck are on the public side; lounges and gate-area shops may be beyond security. Confirm that your flight uses T3: some international services use T2.

From the train to the airline counter

  1. Leave the train at Haneda Airport Terminal 3. T1/T2 is a different stop.
  2. Follow Departures / 3F, using lifts if you have a stroller or large suitcase.
  3. Read the flight display for your flight number and counter. Counter assignments can change.
  4. Complete the airline’s check-in, baggage and document requirements, then follow security and departure immigration signs.
  5. Find your gate and boarding deadline before settling down for food or shopping.

Allow a station-to-counter stage in your journey: platform exit, lifts and locating the airline all take time. We do not give one guaranteed walking time for every train exit and counter. Keikyu’s station-to-terminal diagrams show the approach.

Choose one extra stop, after you know what time is free

Time genuinely spare before your next required procedureOur suggestion
15–20 minutesStay on the departure floor. Find a toilet or drink nearby; avoid another floor or a queue.
30–45 minutesIf still on the public side, choose a short deck visit or browse. These are visit allowances, not time before takeoff.
60 minutes or moreA seated meal can fit if the queue and service time leave room for the next procedure. Choose an open restaurant in the correct area.

Do not subtract these activities from check-in or security time. If already airside, use gate-area facilities; do not plan to return to Edo Ko-ji for dinner. Restaurant opening hours vary even though the terminal is open all night.

Stored bags and lounges: two easy mistakes to avoid

Collect a public-area locker bag before going through departure procedures. The airport’s locker listing gives terminal locations, sizes and charges; availability is not guaranteed. Keep the receipt or locker details accessible rather than packed away.

TIAT LOUNGE is not a public waiting room for anyone arriving at the airport. Entry depends on its location, your departure eligibility and the accepted airline, card or paid-access conditions. Do not assume a card accepted at a domestic POWER LOUNGE also grants TIAT access. A lounge listing’s opening hours do not guarantee admission or space.

If your counter is not open yet

Stay on the public side, keep bags with you or use suitable storage, and choose a nearby open facility. For an overnight wait, the terminal’s entry and overnight rules matter as well as the flight time.
