Is Haneda Airport Open 24 Hours? Overnight & Early Flights

YOUR VISIT, EXPLAINED

Terminal 3 is generally open 24 hours; that does not apply to every Haneda terminal or service. The airport says overnight stays inside T3 are for people who have used or will use international flights. A domestic-only early flight is not a reason to assume you can sleep there.

What is open, and what is not?

Area or serviceWhat the official guidance means for you
T3 terminal buildingGenerally open 24 hours. Overnight eligibility is restricted to international-flight users under the airport’s rules.
T1 and T2 domestic areasGenerally 05:00–24:00, with lobby closure tied to the last flights. You generally cannot stay in the lobby after it closes.
T2 international departuresOpens at 05:00; the departure lobby remains open until after the last departure. Do not use T3’s 24-hour rule for T2.
Airline counters and securityTheir operation depends on the flight and facility. An open terminal does not mean you can check a bag or enter airside at any hour.
Restaurants, lockers and transportEach has its own hours or access conditions. Trains do not run continuously through the night.

Arriving the evening before an early flight

If you are eligible to wait in T3 overnight, expect an airport waiting area, not a reserved bed. Keep your flight details available and follow staff instructions. Seats, quiet surroundings and charging points cannot be guaranteed. For reliable rest, especially with children, compare a bookable hotel and its actual early-morning access to your departure terminal.

The Royal Park Hotel Tokyo Haneda is listed at T3; the airport hotel directory also lists other terminal properties. Choose by access to your departure terminal, not the word “airport” in the hotel name. A shuttle that starts after your check-in target will not solve an early-flight journey. See the airport’s hotel directory.

A flight just after midnight belongs to the next date

Ticket: 31 October, 00:30. Airport-ready target: 30 October, 21:30. Travel to the airport on the evening of 30 October. If you travel there on the evening of 31 October, the flight has already left.

In the planner, enter 31 October at 00:30 as the flight and your actual free time on 30 October. All times on this site use Japan Standard Time (JST). The date is as important as the clock time.

When the first or last train does not fit

For a 06:30 flight, our three-hour planning target would be 03:30. Do not assume a morning train can reach it. Compare the airport-access timetable with your terminal and airline instructions, then choose an eligible overnight wait, suitable accommodation or a prearranged road journey. Road services also need availability and a real pickup time.

A simple plan for food and luggage

Eat before the late-night journey if your preferred terminal restaurant will be closed. Keep medication, baby supplies, documents and a charger with you until baggage drop actually opens. If using a locker or staffed counter, choose one you can reach at the collection time; a storage service on the wrong side of security is not a usable plan.

T3’s observation deck is listed as open 24 hours, subject to weather closure. It is an optional short view, not an indoor sleeping area or a substitute for rest.
