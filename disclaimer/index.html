PLANNING LIMITS

What a departure-day plan can tell you

Before You Fly Japan helps compare a manageable last stop with the journey to your airport. The guide and calculator provide planning guidance. They do not reserve transport or admission, confirm a flight, or guarantee arrival and boarding.

Published facts and planning estimates

We identify official information where available and add editorial allowances for a useful visit, walking, waiting, luggage and terminal movement. A suggested duration is not a live timetable, an observed queue or a promise that a shop will be open. Source and model dates describe the checks carried out; they do not replace current operational notices.

Airline instructions take priority

The planner's three-hour airport-ready default is for international departures and is not a universal airline rule. Check-in, bag-drop and boarding can have separate deadlines. If your airline asks you to arrive earlier, use that earlier time. Domestic flights, connections, special assistance and unusual baggage need the relevant airline's arrangements.

When the optional visit should end

Transport disruption, a storage problem, a long queue or a terminal change can remove spare time. Shorten or abandon sightseeing and use an appropriate current airport route. Recalculating a model cannot create a train service, a vacant locker or an open airline counter.

Access, prices and purchases

Step-free access must be established for the exact station and venue route; a walking allowance does not certify accessibility. Published prices, ticket conditions and opening hours can change. Purchases and bookings are made with the operator under its own terms, and we cannot change them for you.
