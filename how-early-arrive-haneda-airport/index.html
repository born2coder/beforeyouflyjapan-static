DEPARTURE-DAY GUIDE

How Early to Arrive at Haneda for an International Flight

For an international flight, Before You Fly uses 3 hours before departure at the correct terminal, with bags collected as a conservative planning target. This is our allowance, not an airport-wide airline deadline. Your carrier’s earlier arrival instructions take priority.

Which time matters: station, counter or boarding gate?

TimeWhat you need to have done
Airport-ready targetReach the correct terminal with documents and bags ready to begin airline procedures.
Check-in / bag-drop deadlineFinish the procedure required by your airline; online check-in may still leave baggage or document checks.
Boarding deadlineBe at the gate by the airline’s time. This can be well before the scheduled takeoff.

The airport station’s arrival time is not the counter arrival time. Keep the walk through the terminal in the journey calculation. This guide concerns international departures from the city; domestic flights and flight-to-flight connections have separate requirements.

T2 and T3 are different final destinations

Flight terminalRail stop and departure floor
T3Use Haneda Airport Terminal 3 station and follow Departures / 3F.
T2 internationalUse Haneda Airport Terminal 1·2 station on Keikyu, or the Terminal 2 monorail stop, then follow INTERNATIONAL departure signs to 3F.

Some international services use T2. The flight number and airline’s current terminal information determine the destination, not simply “international = T3.” If you arrive at the wrong terminal, use the airport’s transfer guidance and recalculate; do not assume the correction is absorbed automatically. Worked example: what ends at 16:35 and what ends at 15:45

For a 21:00 international flight, these examples protect an 18:00 terminal-ready target. The components below are deliberately rounded planning allowances, not advertised train running times or departures you can book. They assume you are already at the collection point when sightseeing ends; travel back to a distant hotel is additional.

Example and component orderCalculationEnd sightseeing / begin bag collection
Already in Shinagawa: pickup 20 + station/wait 15 + rail allowance 30 + terminal movement 2020 + 15 + 30 + 20 = 85 min16:35
Back at a Shinjuku hotel: pickup 20 + station walk 15 + wait 15 + rail/connections 65 + terminal movement 2020 + 15 + 15 + 65 + 20 = 135 min15:45

These rounded examples show the arithmetic, not fixed travel times from all of Shinagawa or Shinjuku. A distant hotel, lift route or missed connection changes the inputs. Do not count a hotel-to-station walk twice when a live door-to-door itinerary already includes it.

After-midnight example: use the previous date

For 21 November, 00:30, the target is 20 November, 21:30. Subtracting an illustrative 90-minute journey and 20-minute pickup gives 19:40 on 20 November as the end of sightseeing. The planner accepts the flight and free time on different dates and displays JST.

When three hours is not the right target

An earlier airline instruction, assistance arrangement or substantial disruption takes priority. A shorter check-in deadline printed by the airline is not a recommendation to arrive at the last possible minute. The purpose of this allowance is to leave room for terminal navigation and variable queues.

If your airline asks you to arrive earlier: use that earlier terminal-ready time. The current planner uses a fixed three-hour international allowance and has no field for an earlier airline target. Do not accept a suggested route unless it also fits the earlier requirement; shorten it or go directly to the airport.
