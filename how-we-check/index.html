OUR METHOD

How we check our information

Most travel guides help you decide where to go. Before You Fly helps you decide what you can still safely do before your flight—and exactly when the optional part must end.

Sources we prioritize

  • Airport official sites for terminals, departure procedures and airport access.
  • Airlines for check-in, baggage-drop, boarding and special-assistance deadlines.
  • Railway and transport operators for routes, service status and station information.
  • Official attractions, markets, district organizations and local tourism offices for access, closures and operating context.

What the timing includes

Travel time

More than the train ride

Walking to the station, finding the platform, waiting, transfers and the airport-station-to-terminal movement all matter.

Luggage

Drop, return and retrieval

Storage is not treated as instant. Plans include a conservative allowance and reject cross-region luggage detours.

Waiting time

Queues are real time

Food, ticketing, lockers, lifts and crowded station movement can reduce the optional window.

Airport buffer

Protected from sightseeing

The planner works backwards from an airport-ready time. Optional activity never consumes the airline-procedure buffer.

How a plan is reviewed

  1. Confirm the place and official source are active.
  2. Check operating context, closure patterns and practical access.
  3. Build each step with walking, waiting and transfer allowances.
  4. Calculate backwards from the protected airport-ready time.
  5. Test starting-area, luggage and interest combinations.
  6. Check the plan page, leave-by display and official links on mobile and desktop.

Our risk labels

Time Risk
The chance that queues, walking or a long transfer will erode the optional window.
Luggage Risk
How much a large bag, storage search or separate pickup can complicate the route.
Transfer Complexity
The number and difficulty of station, line and airport-terminal movements.
Minimum Useful Time
The shortest verified window in which the experience plus protected airport transfer can work.

Last checked does not mean guaranteed

Every reviewed page shows a date, but transport, weather, crowds, closures and airline instructions can change afterwards. Recheck the airline, airport, live transport and venue before leaving the final area.

Correction policy

If an official source changes or a route no longer works as described, we revise or remove the recommendation. Travelers can report a specific correction through the contact page.
