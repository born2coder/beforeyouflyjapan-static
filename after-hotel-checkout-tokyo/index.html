DEPARTURE-DAY GUIDE

What to Do After Hotel Checkout in Tokyo Before a Flight

After checkout, the best last stop is often the one on the same side of Tokyo as your bags and airport train. A relaxed meal in Shinagawa can be more useful than another famous district if the suitcase is waiting there.

Choose the bag location before choosing the neighborhood

Where the bags will beA practical final stopWhat makes it work
Hotel near ShinjukuA nearby meal, Shinjuku shops or Hanazono ShrineFinish near the hotel, collect once, then take the airport route. Going to Ginza and returning west adds a second city journey.
ShinagawaA station-side meal or ecute purchase if you already have valid JR gate accessCollect bags, then reach the separate Keikyu gates for Haneda. ecute is inside JR gates; it is not an outside-gate waiting room.
Tokyo StationMarunouchi facade or Tokyo Character StreetStay on the correct side of the station for storage and your train. The Marunouchi and Yaesu sides are not interchangeable.
Ueno / Keisei-UenoA short Ameyoko pass or Ueno Park stopUseful for a Keisei airport return. JR Ueno and Keisei-Ueno are separate stations; collect near the operator you will use.

Hotel storage may be convenient, but ask about the collection deadline and whether staff can accept the actual bag. Do not assume every hotel offers free storage after checkout.

One documented Tokyo Station option

GRANSTA lists a B1 cloakroom inside JR gates and a 1F JR EAST Travel Service Center cloakroom outside the gates. Its published S/M/L fees are ¥500/¥1,000/¥2,000 per item. The B1 listing gives 08:30–21:00 and a separate same-day storage note of “until 20:30”; the outside-gate counter lists 08:30–20:00. Plan collection before the applicable cutoff rather than treating the building’s closing time as spare sightseeing time.

The operator’s page is dated 23 May 2025; size/weight limits and current counter terms still apply. Open the storage map and published conditions. An available small locker is not a substitute for a compartment that fits your suitcase.

Haneda and Narita lead to different last-day choices

For Haneda, Shinagawa → Keikyu or the Hamamatsucho monorail corridor can keep the route compact. For Narita, compare a reserved Narita Express from a station on its route with Keisei from Keisei-Ueno or Nippori. Choose the departure station before lunch, not after collecting a heavy bag.

If your Narita bags are still in Shinjuku, an eastern Tokyo stop is only useful when the return to Shinjuku is budgeted. Narita town is a separate excursion; do not add it merely because its name matches the airport.

When the final stop should simply be lunch

If storage is full, rain makes the walk unpleasant or the hotel pickup has slipped, choose a seated meal near the departure station and keep the case with you. Tokyo Station food shopping, an outside-gate Shinagawa meal or the airport itself are complete options. You do not need another attraction for the day to feel finished.

If your airline asks you to arrive earlier: use that earlier terminal-ready time. The current planner uses a fixed three-hour international allowance and has no field for an earlier airline target. Do not accept a suggested route unless it also fits the earlier requirement; shorten it or go directly to the airport.
