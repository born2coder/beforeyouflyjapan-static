EXPERIENCE STOP

Tokyo Station & Marunouchi

Stand between Tokyo’s restored red-brick gateway and the glass towers of Marunouchi—a vivid last look at how the city layers history and modern life.

Look for: View the facade from the plaza before going inside, then look for regional food and gifts that bring the rest of Japan into one station.
