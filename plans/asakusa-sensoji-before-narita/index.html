Why this experience is worth your last hours in Tokyo

Visit Sensoji and Nakamise, then use the east-Tokyo corridor toward Narita without crossing back west. The plan keeps the experience focused in one area so that the leave-by time remains easy to follow.

EXPERIENCE STOP

Asakusa & Sensoji

Follow the classic approach from Kaminarimon through Nakamise to Sensoji, experiencing temple ritual, traditional shopping and old-Tokyo street life in one clear sequence.
