WHY THIS ROUTE

Why this experience is worth your last hours in Tokyo

This route connects Ginza and Ginza Evening in a practical order, letting each stop reveal a different side of the city while the final movement stays pointed toward the airport.

What you’ll remember: Ginza’s precise service, specialist retail and urban polish

EXPERIENCE STOP

Ginza

Experience Tokyo at its most polished: historic department stores, precise service, Japanese craft and modern architecture in one walkable district.

Look for: Step into a depachika food hall or long-established specialty shop; the care in wrapping, display and service is as memorable as the purchase.
