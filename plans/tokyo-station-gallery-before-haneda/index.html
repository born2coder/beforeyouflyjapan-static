DATED WORKED EXAMPLE · ALL TIMES JST

Example timeline · Oct 10, 11:30 PM HND flight

Worked example for 10 October 2026, beginning in Tokyo Station with no luggage detour. It illustrates the checked model and is not a current opening-hours or train-service promise. Enter your actual travel date to check availability.

This gallery example uses the exhibition calendar verified for 19 September–8 November 2026. The planner will not offer this visit outside that window until the calendar is reviewed. The station exterior remains a separate alternative.

4:00 PM

Walk to Tokyo Station Gallery family art stop

Use the Tokyo Station Gallery entrance by the Marunouchi North Exit. The interior requires exhibition admission; follow the checked exhibition calendar, and use the station facade as the alternative when it is closed.

Planning allowance: 15 minutes

4:15 PM

Visit Tokyo Station Gallery

Choose one exhibition section and look at the historic brick walls. Use the lift where needed and ask staff about stroller access. Ticket availability is checked on arrival.

Planning allowance: 60 minutes

5:15 PM

Use toilets, diaper facilities and a seated break

The gallery lists a 2F diaper station, a lift and stroller loans. Nursing space is not promised here; ask staff or use station services.

Planning allowance: 30 minutes

5:45 PM

Return to Tokyo Station station area

Return to the station entrance saved for your Tokyo Station airport connection. Pick the correct operator and gate side rather than the nearest station-name pin; any bag collection is shown separately when selected.

Planning allowance: 15 minutes

6:00 PM

Head to Haneda Airport

Compare the route via Shinagawa and Keikyu with the Tokyo Monorail via Hamamatsucho from the actual last stop. Check your Haneda terminal station and the train destination; a rail ride alone is not the full allowance. The 90 minutes shown are the route’s planning allowance, including its assigned walking, waiting and connections; they are not a published train ride or guaranteed departure.

Planning allowance: 90 minutes

7:30 PM

Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Planning allowance: 30 minutes

Complete route ends: Oct 10, 8:00 PM JST. Any time remaining before the airport-ready target stays spare.

Opening hours and when to cut the visit

10:00–18:00, Fridays to 20:00; last admission 30 minutes before closing. The planner conservatively finishes by 18:00. Verified exhibition calendar: 19 September–8 November 2026; Mondays closed except 21 September, 12 October and 2 November. 13 October closed.
