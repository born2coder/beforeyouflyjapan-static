WHY THIS ROUTE

Why this experience is worth your last hours in Japan

Move from the working food streets of Tsukiji to the polished avenues of Ginza. One direct walk reveals two very different faces of central Tokyo without wasting time on backtracking.

What you’ll remember: Tokyo’s working food culture followed by its polished commercial streets

EXPERIENCE STOP

Tsukiji Outer Market

A dense food district of seafood, produce, knives, pantry goods and small places to eat.

Look for: Choose one or two things to eat well instead of queuing for everything, and watch how specialist shops focus on a single craft or ingredient.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Ginza

Central Tokyo’s best-known district for department stores, specialist shops, dining and modern architecture.

Look for: Step into a depachika food hall or long-established specialty shop; the care in wrapping, display and service is as memorable as the purchase.

See why this place is worth your time →

Do not choose this plan if:

  • Your available time is shorter than the minimum shown.
  • Live transport is disrupted or your airline asks you to arrive earlier.
  • You still need to carry large luggage through the sightseeing area.
