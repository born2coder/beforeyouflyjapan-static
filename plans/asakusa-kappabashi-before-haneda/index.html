WHY THIS ROUTE

Why this experience is worth your last hours in Tokyo

Begin with the ceremonial route from Kaminarimon to Sensoji, then continue into the professional kitchenware culture of Kappabashi. The walk moves from public tradition to the tools behind everyday Japanese food.

What you’ll remember: the changing approach from Kaminarimon and Nakamise to Sensoji’s sacred space

EXPERIENCE STOP

Asakusa & Sensoji

Follow the classic approach from Kaminarimon through Nakamise to Sensoji, experiencing temple ritual, traditional shopping and old-Tokyo street life in one clear sequence.

Look for: Move beyond the first photo at Kaminarimon: watch the route change from shopping street to sacred space as the main hall comes into view.

EXPERIENCE STOP

Kappabashi

Browse the tools that shape Japanese food culture—from chef’s knives and ceramics to astonishingly realistic plastic food models.

Look for: Look at how each shop specializes in one professional craft; even without buying, the precision reveals the unseen work behind a Japanese meal.
