WHY THIS ROUTE

Why this experience is worth your last hours in Japan

This route connects Asakusa & Sensoji and Ueno & Ameyoko and Akihabara in a practical order, letting each stop reveal a different side of the city while the final movement stays pointed toward the airport.

What you’ll remember: the changing approach from Kaminarimon and Nakamise to Sensoji’s sacred space

EXPERIENCE STOP

Asakusa & Sensoji

A temple district centered on Kaminarimon, Nakamise and Sensoji’s main hall.

Look for: Move beyond the first photo at Kaminarimon: watch the route change from shopping street to sacred space as the main hall comes into view.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Ueno & Ameyoko

A broad cultural park beside a lively railway-side market district.

Look for: Pay attention to the transition from the open park to the railway-side market—the abrupt change in sound, scale and pace is the heart of this stop.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Akihabara

A district of electronics, games, anime, hobby collecting and specialist multi-floor stores.

Look for: Do not judge a building by the ground floor; choose one interest and explore upward, where increasingly specialized collections reveal the district’s depth.

See why this place is worth your time →

Is this plan right for you?

Starting areas
Tokyo, Ueno, Asakusa
Time needed
455 min minimum; 485 min recommended
Start window
07:30–10:30 JST · Daily
Luggage
Store large luggage before starting this plan.

Choose this plan if:

  • You have at least 8 hours before your recommended airport arrival time.
  • You are starting in one of the listed areas.
  • You want a focused final Tokyo experience with a clear leave-by time.

Do not choose this plan if:

  • Your available time is shorter than the minimum shown.
  • Live transport is disrupted or your airline asks you to arrive earlier.
  • You still need to carry large luggage through the sightseeing area.
