Opening hours and when to cut the visit

11:00–18:00; last admission 17:30. Mondays closed; a Monday national holiday opens and the following day closes. Holiday exceptions verified through 2027. Adult admission currently ¥700.

The museum occupies multiple floors; a step-free interior route has not been verified. Hozenji is optional and should be skipped if delayed.

Cut optional shopping, workshop or shrine-counter queues first. If a locker is unavailable, skip the visit and take the airport route with your bags. Preserve the three-hour airport buffer.
