Opening hours and when to cut the visit

Exhibition 9:30–17:00; last admission 16:30. Closed second / fourth Mondays except national holidays, and 29 December–3 January; 2–3 January have special exhibition opening. Holiday exceptions are verified through 2027.

Store bags at Tokyo Station before leaving and return there to collect them. Nursing is available on 1F with staff assistance; museum lift access is confirmed, but the complete station route is not certified step-free.

Cut optional shopping, workshop or shrine-counter queues first. If a locker is unavailable, skip the visit and take the airport route with your bags. Preserve the three-hour airport buffer.
