DATED WORKED EXAMPLE · ALL TIMES JST

Example timeline · Oct 10, 11:30 PM KIX flight

Worked example for 10 October 2026, beginning in Namba with no luggage detour. It illustrates the checked model and is not a current opening-hours or train-service promise. Enter your actual travel date to check availability.

4:00 PM

Walk to Hozenji temple and alley

Find Hozenji’s small temple space and the neighboring stone-paved alley, then return toward Nankai Namba. Stay considerate of worshippers and restaurant entrances; a long meal is not included in this short walk.

Planning allowance: 15 minutes

4:15 PM

Visit Hozenji and one short alley segment

Look at the moss-covered Mizukake Fudo and stone-paved lane. Follow posted prayer guidance and respect worshippers. Keep this a temple visit, rather than a restaurant queue.

Planning allowance: 30 minutes

4:45 PM

Return to Namba station area

Return to the station entrance saved for your Namba airport connection. Pick the correct operator and gate side rather than the nearest station-name pin; any bag collection is shown separately when selected.

Planning allowance: 20 minutes

5:05 PM

Head to Kansai Airport

Use the Nankai airport route from Nankai Namba. Check the train destination and your terminal; after Kansai-airport Station, T2 needs the Aeroplaza shuttle. The 90 minutes shown are the route’s planning allowance, including its assigned walking, waiting and connections; they are not a published train ride or guaranteed departure.

Planning allowance: 90 minutes

6:35 PM

Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Allow 30 minutes for airport station access / Terminal 2 shuttle navigation, plus 20 minutes of spare time. Terminal 1 passengers retain the spare time.

Planning allowance: 50 minutes

Complete route ends: Oct 10, 7:25 PM JST. Any time remaining before the airport-ready target stays spare.

Opening hours and when to cut the visit

Grounds are accessible 24 hours; the office is 10:00–18:00. The planner uses a 9:00–17:00 daylight grounds window and does not promise counter service.

Stone paving can be slippery in rain. This is not a verified stroller / low-walking route.

Cut optional shopping, workshop or shrine-counter queues first. If a locker is unavailable, skip the visit and take the airport route with your bags. Preserve the three-hour airport buffer.
