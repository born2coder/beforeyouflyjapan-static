WHY THIS ROUTE

What this route gives you

Keep the visit around the Ginza 4-chome crossing and one food or shopping destination such as Ginza Mitsukoshi. A restaurant queue is a reason to shorten the browse, not delay the airport trip.

EXPERIENCE STOP

Shiodome

Walk a quieter vertical district of elevated paths, modern towers and public art—a useful glimpse of Tokyo’s carefully engineered cityscape.

Look for: Notice how pedestrians, trains and roads are layered at different heights, then use a cafe stop to slow the final day down.
