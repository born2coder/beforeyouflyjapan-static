WHY THIS ROUTE

Why this experience is worth your last hours in Tokyo

Enter Narita through its traditional temple approach, visit the main complex, then return through the same town for food or a small gift. The entire experience stays close to the airport.

What you’ll remember: a traditional temple-town experience within safe reach of Narita Airport

EXPERIENCE STOP

Naritasan Shinshoji

End the trip in a living temple town close to Narita, following a historic approach to a vast Buddhist complex without returning to central Tokyo.

Look for: Continue beyond the first hall if time allows; the scale and layered buildings make this feel like a full cultural experience, not an airport-side compromise.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Narita Omotesando

Walk a traditional temple approach lined with crafts, sweets and eel restaurants—a final taste of old Japan within practical reach of Narita Airport.

Look for: Notice the eel restaurants preparing food near the street and the way the road gradually reveals the temple district below.

Do not choose this plan if:
