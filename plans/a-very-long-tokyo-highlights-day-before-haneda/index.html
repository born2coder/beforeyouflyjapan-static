WHY THIS ROUTE

Why this experience is worth your last hours in Japan

This route connects Tokyo Station & Marunouchi and Nihonbashi and Tsukiji Outer Market and Ginza and Odaiba in a practical order, letting each stop reveal a different side of the city while the final movement stays pointed toward the airport.

What you’ll remember: a focused experience of Tokyo Station & Marunouchi

EXPERIENCE STOP

Tokyo Station & Marunouchi

Tokyo’s principal intercity station and the business district facing its restored Marunouchi building.

Look for: View the facade from the plaza before going inside, then look for regional food and gifts that bring the rest of Japan into one station.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Nihonbashi

A historic bridge and merchant district east of Tokyo Station.

Look for: Look for road-history details on and around the bridge, then compare centuries-old shop names with the modern city built around them.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Tsukiji Outer Market

A dense food district of seafood, produce, knives, pantry goods and small places to eat.

Look for: Choose one or two things to eat well instead of queuing for everything, and watch how specialist shops focus on a single craft or ingredient.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Ginza

Central Tokyo’s best-known district for department stores, specialist shops, dining and modern architecture.

Look for: Step into a depachika food hall or long-established specialty shop; the care in wrapping, display and service is as memorable as the purchase.

See why this place is worth your time →

EXPERIENCE STOP

Odaiba

A spacious Tokyo Bay district of waterfront walks, views and large indoor facilities.

Look for: Walk to the water before entering a mall; the view back toward central Tokyo is what makes Odaiba more than a shopping stop.

See why this place is worth your time →

Choose this plan if:

  • You have at least 11 hours before your recommended airport arrival time.
  • You are starting in one of the listed areas.
  • You want a focused final Tokyo experience with a clear leave-by time.

Do not choose this plan if:

  • Your available time is shorter than the minimum shown.
  • Live transport is disrupted or your airline asks you to arrive earlier.
  • You still need to carry large luggage through the sightseeing area.
