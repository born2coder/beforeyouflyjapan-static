Choose this plan if:

  • You can begin in Narita Airport.

  • You are willing to shorten or skip the local visit when a queue or transport change uses the spare time.

Do not choose this plan if:

  • Your starting area or hotel collection route is not supported by the calculation.
  • Your airline requires an earlier arrival, or the live journey is longer than the allowance.
