DATED WORKED EXAMPLE · ALL TIMES JST

Example timeline · Oct 10, 11:30 PM HND flight

Worked example for 10 October 2026, beginning in Ueno with no luggage detour. It illustrates the checked model and is not a current opening-hours or train-service promise. Enter your actual travel date to check availability.

2:50 PM

Walk to Ueno Toshogu

Use Ueno Park’s approach to Ueno Toshogu. Distinguish the public approach from the paid area and its closing time; if paid entry no longer fits, keep the visit outside.

Planning allowance: 25 minutes

3:15 PM

Visit Ueno Toshogu paid inner precinct

Look at the gold leaf, carved gate and stone lantern approach. Shrine interiors are not open to visitors. Skip a ticket queue rather than extend the visit.

Planning allowance: 45 minutes

4:00 PM

Return to Ueno station area

Return to the station entrance saved for your Ueno airport connection. Pick the correct operator and gate side rather than the nearest station-name pin; any bag collection is shown separately when selected.

Planning allowance: 20 minutes

4:20 PM

Head to Haneda Airport

Compare the route via Shinagawa and Keikyu with the Tokyo Monorail via Hamamatsucho from the actual last stop. Check your Haneda terminal station and the train destination; a rail ride alone is not the full allowance. The 90 minutes shown are the route’s planning allowance, including its assigned walking, waiting and connections; they are not a published train ride or guaranteed departure.

Planning allowance: 90 minutes

5:50 PM

Find your departure terminal and keep a disruption margin

Confirm the check-in floor and airline counter. Keep 30 minutes for terminal navigation and a missed connection; do not spend it on another stop.

Planning allowance: 30 minutes

Complete route ends: Oct 10, 6:20 PM JST. Any time remaining before the airport-ready target stays spare.

Opening hours and when to cut the visit

Paid precinct: 9:30–16:00 October–February; 9:30–17:00 March–September. Adult admission currently ¥700; grounds and paid precinct have different hours.

Weather or ceremonies can close the paid area. Park paths add walking; this route is not offered as a low-walking option.

Cut optional shopping, workshop or shrine-counter queues first. If a locker is unavailable, skip the visit and take the airport route with your bags. Preserve the three-hour airport buffer.
